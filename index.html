Absence Cap Rules
  • Max 4 physicians absent simultaneously (5+ = flag → Eskedar → Dr. Pham)
  • Codes that count: VAC, EL, LOA
  • Excluded: SL, OFF, ET, AT, IPS, IW, NC, VV (Virtual Visit)
  • Holiday wheel days shown in purple — pre-approved, no alert triggered
  • NP/PA cap: max 1 staff NP/PA out at a time — tracked separately
How to Use
  • Open the Scheduling Suite (recommended) or an individual tool above
  • Drag and drop provider .xlsm schedule files onto the upload zone
  • Click Compile Schedule to generate the monthly calendar
  • Switch to Room Assignment tab — absences carry over automatically
  • Export to CSV or print directly from any view
